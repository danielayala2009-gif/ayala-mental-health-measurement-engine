# Ayala Mental Health Measurement Engine

Research and clinical decision-support prototype integrating 17 mental-health and suicide-related measures within an IMV-informed framework.

## Version 0.1

This first version provides:

- A registry of 17 measures identified from the source literature.
- Manual score entry for each instrument.
- Instrument-specific scoring metadata and expected ranges where established.
- IMV-oriented grouping into pre-motivational, motivational, ideation/intention, and volitional domains.
- Conservative suicide-safety guardrails: the app does **not** produce a suicide probability or declare someone safe/low risk.
- Licensing safeguards: copyrighted/restricted item wording is not embedded.
- A structured profile output for research and clinician review.

## Important limitation

This repository is an educational/research prototype, not a diagnostic tool or medical device. Questionnaire scores cannot determine whether an individual is safe, and any indication of current suicidal ideation, intent, planning, or recent suicidal behavior requires direct human assessment according to applicable clinical and emergency protocols.

## Measures in the initial registry

1. Beck Scale for Suicide Ideation (BSS/BSSI)
2. Adult Psychiatric Morbidity Survey suicide-history items
3. Beck Depression Inventory-II (BDI-II)
4. Perceived Stress Scale – 4 item (PSS-4)
5. Warwick–Edinburgh Mental Well-Being Scale (WEMWBS-14)
6. Defeat Scale
7. Entrapment Scale
8. ENRICHD Social Support Instrument (ESSI)
9. Socially Prescribed Perfectionism subscale
10. Interpersonal Needs Questionnaire (INQ-12)
11. Goal Adjustment Scale
12. Life Orientation Test – Revised (LOT-R)
13. Connor–Davidson Resilience Scale – 10 item (CD-RISC-10)
14. Acquired Capability for Suicide Scale (ACSS; version-specific)
15. Barratt Impulsiveness Scale Version 11 (BIS-11)
16. Exposure to Suicide / Self-Harm items
17. Suicide / Self-Harm Mental Imagery items
